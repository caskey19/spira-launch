# Revision 5 — closure and paper finish

The model now has a rounded main-lid edge, integrated recessed grip fluting, a domed drinking-cap crown, a shallow molded cap seat and a smooth drinking rim. The small cap sits closer to the deck and is offset toward the drinking edge. Both helical thread studies use a coarser 3.2 mm pitch; the drinking cap uses 1.5 turns. The lighter, more restrained fiber material and shallower exterior contours follow the refined molded-paper direction.

At the selected setting: 195 mm closed height, 90 mm maximum diameter, 24 mm drink opening, 1 mm exterior groove, 10 mm inward rib depth and 3 mm base wall. The numerical cavity estimate is approximately 844 mL, not a measured fill rating. The solid rib crest is 12 mm thick and needs supplier reassessment for material efficiency and manufacture.

The intended use cycle is one prepared shake. The next development milestone is a prototype and supplier feasibility feedback. This revision does not establish manufacturability, sealing, mixing performance, food-contact suitability, 24-hour containment or whole-assembly industrial compostability.

Geometry checks cover the closed envelope, aperture continuity, hollow cap and rim contact, finite cutaway geometry, absence of the former hinge/plug/latch and sampled nominal spout-thread clearance. The detailed review record stays with the editable product study; this document makes no production-release claim.

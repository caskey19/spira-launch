# Spira revision 4 — flush main lid and screw spout cap

Founder annotation: “lets change the main lid so instead of it looking like a bulky cap that hangs over the bottle lets make it one that fits just right over the top and we ave t make the flip clouser instead of it \"plugging\" the whole that its just a cap that screws into the spout”.

The screw closure is interpreted as a hollow cap that threads **over the outside of the drinking spout**. Its roof meets the spout rim; no plug enters the drink bore. This keeps the selected 24 mm passage clear when open. The small cap removes completely; a tether has not been added or assumed.

The large lid now matches the body's maximum 90 mm diameter. A recessed neck and body-aligned collar replace the overhanging cap silhouette. The large threaded opening remains available for adding powder and water. The small screw cap replaces the hinge, axle, latch and drinking-port plug. There is no separate air vent to leave unsealed; air must return through the drink opening during sipping. Drinking flow requires physical testing.

Selected geometry: 195 mm closed assembled height, 90 mm body diameter, 10 mm inner rib depth, 1.6 mm shallow outer groove, two helix turns, 3 mm base wall, 24 mm drink opening. The review retains the selected 3 mm main-lid separation as an exploded-view setting; assemble at zero separation to inspect the closed envelope. All components retain the matching natural fiber appearance.

Numerical cavity integration is approximately 844 mL. This is gross modeled cavity, not a measured fill rating: the intended 16–20 US fl oz liquid serving also needs allowance for powder and mixing headspace. The solid rib crest remains 11.4 mm thick and needs a supplier assessment for lightweight manufacturable rib construction.

Geometry checks confirmed the closed envelope, non-overhanging collar, actual thread geometry, hollow cap, unobstructed open passage, absence of the previous plug/hinge/latch, independent inside/outside relief and finite section geometry. These are model checks only. No leakage, torque, thread-wear, barrier, drop, food-contact, mixing or compostability test has been performed. Secure vigorous shaking, whole-assembly industrial compostability and 24-hour containment under refrigerated and room-temperature conditions remain requirements, not established performance. No conventional plastic liner or silicone gasket is silently assumed.
